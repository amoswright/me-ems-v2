EMT
  1. Manage airway as appropriate, see Airway Algorithm, Blue 2
  2. If shock present, refer to Medical Shock protocol, Gold 14
  3. If anaphylaxis is identified, assist administration of patient's own EPINEPHrine auto-injector, administer an adult or pediatric (as applicable) auto-injector, OR provide EPINEPHrine through the Maine EMS Check and Inject program in the anterolateral thigh:
    • Adult: EPINEPHrine 0.3 mg IM [0.3 mL of 1 mg/mL] in anterolateral thigh
    • Pediatric <25 kg: 0.15 mg IM; >25 kg: 0.3 mg IM in anterolateral thigh
    • Repeat IM EPINEPHrine doses every 5 min, as needed
    • Notify OLMC of incoming critical patient
  4. Request ALS, if available
  5. If wheezing persists 5 minutes after first dose of EPINEPHrine, refer to Respiratory Distress with Bronchospasm protocol, Blue 6.
  6. Consider local measures to prevent absorption of allergen
Advanced EMT
  1. If anaphylaxis identified:
    • Adult: EPINEPHrine 0.3 mg IM [0.3 mL of 1 mg/mL] in anterolateral thigh
    • Pediatric <25 kg: 0.15 mg IM; >25 kg: 0.3 mg IM in anterolateral thigh
    • Repeat IM EPINEPHrine doses every 5 min; if repeated, request ALS
  2. IV en route
  3. Cardiac monitor
  4. If shock present, administer fluid bolus
  5. Minor symptoms / single-dose resolution → may cancel ALS with OLMC approval
Paramedic
  1. Consider glucagon 1 mg IV q 5 minutes for patients on beta-blockers not responding to EPINEPHrine
  2. Diphenhydramine (Benadryl):
    • Adult: 25–50 mg IV/IO/IM
    • Pediatric: 1–2 mg/kg IV/IO/IM (max single dose 50 mg)
  3. Consider ONE of the following; repeat every 5 minutes as needed:
    • Albuterol 2.5 mg by nebulization
    • 1 mL of 1 mg/mL EPINEPHrine nebulized with 2 mL NS
    • 0.5 mL of 2.25% racemic EPINEPHrine solution nebulized with 2.5 mL NS
